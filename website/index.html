How it works

From URL to full report in seconds

Submit & queue

The API normalizes your URL, checks the result cache, and enqueues a durable scan job.

Instrument & navigate

An isolated browser context with a rotated fingerprint loads the page under deep instrumentation — network, DOM, and runtime all captured.

Detect in parallel

Five detectors analyze the collected artifact simultaneously, merging results with confidence scoring and conflict resolution.

Persist & deliver

Evidence lands in storage, the report becomes queryable, and a signed webhook notifies you — or poll until done.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does AuthScope bypass captchas or bot protection?

No. AuthScope strictly detects and reports. It never solves captchas, evades WAFs, or automates logins — it tells you what defenses exist so you can plan accordingly.

Is scanning a website with AuthScope legal?

AuthScope performs passive detection only — the same signals visible to any browser visiting the page. It's designed for auditing sites you own, have permission to test, or for lawful research purposes. Users are responsible for complying with applicable terms of service and laws.

What happens if a site blocks the scan?

A blocked first attempt triggers one retry from a different geographic exit. If still blocked, the scan completes as waf_blocked — which is itself valuable intelligence about the target's defenses.

How current are the detection signatures?

Signatures are declarative YAML files with version tracking, hot-reloaded into workers without deploys. New providers, captchas, and WAFs can be added and go live within minutes.

Can I integrate reports into my own systems?

Yes — every scan is delivered via HMAC-SHA256-signed webhooks with automatic retries and a dead-letter queue, or via simple polling against the REST API. Reports are plain JSON.
